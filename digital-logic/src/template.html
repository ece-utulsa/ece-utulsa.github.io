How to

Drag from a pin to a pin to wire. Move a wire: drag its middle segment. Tee off: click a wire, then drag the amber handle beside it to an input — or drag from an input pin and drop it on any wire. Subsystem: press Make subsystem, drag a box around a circuit and name it: the circuit becomes one block. Its switches become the block's inputs, its probes and LEDs its outputs, and wires crossing the box become pins too. Ungroup puts it back. Click a switch to toggle it; hold a button. Drag parts to move, or nudge the selected part with the arrow keys (Shift = 5 steps); R rotates the selected part (Shift+R counter-clockwise). Double-click to rename. C cycles a selected LED's color. Del removes the selection; right-click removes a part or wire. Drag empty board to pan, wheel to zoom. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Touch: drag with one finger, pinch with two to zoom, double-tap to rename, press and hold a part or wire to remove it.
