Two Generators Sharing Real and Reactive Power

ECE 3033 · Lecture 5 · v1.2.1
Model

Two identical three-phase, Y-connected units: 4 MVA, 4.16 kV, Vφ = 4,160/√3 = 2,402 V, rated IA = 555.1 A, ZS = 0.2 + j4.5 Ω in each phase. Load on slides 1 to 4: 5.0 MW at 0.80 lagging, so Qload = 3.75 MVAR and IL = 867.4∠−36.9° A. Slide 5 has its own load, 5.6 MW at 0.85 lagging.

Governors (slides 1 and 2): straight droop lines with sP = 2.25 MW/Hz (3 % speed droop at fnl = 61.00 Hz, 4 MW full load; 2.2514 before rounding). Pk = sP(fnl,k − fsys) and P1 + P2 = Pload. Moving a set point slides the line parallel to itself; sP does not change. Every result is computed with unrounded values and then rounded for display (MW, MVAR and A to 0.1; Hz and power factor to 0.01; V to 1; angles to 0.1°), so the last digit can differ by one from a hand calculation with the rounded numbers.

Fields (slides 3 to 5): the terminal voltage is held at 4.16 kV and taken as the reference. Each unit's current is IAk = (Pk − jQk) / 3Vφ, the currents add to IL, and EAk = Vφ + IAkZS. No saturation; the field current is not computed, only the EA it must produce. On slide 4 each dashed line is the exact path of that unit's EA tip while its P is fixed. Its height, EA sin δ, is ≈ fixed by P: with RA it changes by 4 to 6 % across the slider's range.

Steady state only: no governor, rotor or voltage-regulator dynamics, no losses other than RA in EA. Currents and voltages are drawn on separate diagrams, each with one fixed scale. The circuit on slides 3 and 5 is a schematic.

1 of 5 · Two units, one load

No infinite bus: the units set f and V themselves

The governor set points decide the frequency and the split of P. The field currents decide the terminal voltage and the split of Q.

Set point fnl: the frequency a unit runs at with no load. Droop slope sP: the extra power a unit gives for each hertz the frequency falls, 2.25 MW/Hz here.

3 of 5 · The circuit and the currents

The currents add at the node; each EA follows its own current

The load fixes IL. Set G1's current, and G2 must supply the rest.

Both units: ZS = 0.2 + j4.5 Ω. Terminal voltage Vφ = 2,402 V, the reference.

4 of 5 · The fields share Q

The set points fix P; the fields share Q

Raise G1's field and lower G2's together: Q moves from G2 to G1, while P and the terminal voltage stay put.

Each tip's height, EA sin δ, is fixed by that unit's P, so a tip can only slide sideways. Longer EA: more field current, more Q.
