Two Generators Sharing Real and Reactive Power

ECE 3033 · Lecture 5 · v2.0.0
Model

Two identical units, G1 and G2: 4 MVA, 4.16 kV Y, ZS = 0.2 + j4.5 Ω per phase. They feed one load and nothing else. The terminal voltage Vφ = 2,401.8 V is held and is the reference for every phasor.

Real power: each governor has 3 % droop, a slope sP = 2.25 MW/Hz, and a set point fnl (61.00 Hz by default). Pk = sP(fnl,k − fsys), and the two powers add up to the load.

Reactive power: IA = (P − jQ) / 3Vφ, EA = Vφ + IAZS, and IA2 = IL − IA1. The load is at 0.80 lagging unless a slide says otherwise. RA is kept, so each constant-P line tilts slightly.

Steady state only: a change takes effect at once. The 4 MVA rating and motoring (P below 0) raise a warning; nothing trips. Every diagram has a fixed scale. Currents and voltages are never drawn on one diagram.
