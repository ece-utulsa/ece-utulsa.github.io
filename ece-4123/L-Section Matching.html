Realization

A short narrow (high-Z) line acts as a series inductor, βℓ ≈ X/Zh; a short wide (low-Z) line acts as a shunt capacitor, βℓ ≈ B·Zl. Only the low-pass solutions (series L, shunt C) can be built this way. The chart plays the tuned lengths, which land exactly; the dashed arcs are the lumped moves they replace.
Line model: Hammerstad–Jensen Z₀ and εeff with strip thickness, Kirschning–Jansen dispersion, copper conductor loss (skin effect, current crowding, roughness) and dielectric loss. Not modelled: the step-junction parasitics, radiation and coupling — those need an EM solver.
Finite Q adds series loss to each part: it does not move the design values, but it shows up in the performance. Leave blank for ideal parts.
