THE MEASURES

Four numbers from the task counts

SCHEDULE VARIANCE

SV = EV − PV

7 − 9 = −2 tasks

Negative: behind schedule

SCHEDULE PERFORMANCE INDEX

SPI = EV ÷ PV

7 ÷ 9 = 0.78

Below 1: progress is 78% of plan

START AND FINISH RATIOS

Started ÷ planned · Finished ÷ planned

8 ÷ 10 = 0.80 · 6 ÷ 8 = 0.75

Both below 1: behind on starting and finishing

PERCENT COMPLETE

% complete = EV ÷ BAC

7 ÷ 40 = 18%

Planned by now: 9 ÷ 40 = 23%

IN INDUSTRY

What changes in your first job

Senior Design this year

  • Unit: tasks (work packages)
  • Earned by the 50/50 rule
  • No time cards
  • Measures schedule: SV, SPI, forecast finish

Industry practice

  • Unit: budgeted hours or dollars
  • Earned by the same rules
  • Time cards give actual cost, AC
  • Adds cost: CV, CPI, estimate at completion

Same idea, different unit. In your first job you will add hours, time cards and cost; the cost equations are in Lecture 3 for reference.
