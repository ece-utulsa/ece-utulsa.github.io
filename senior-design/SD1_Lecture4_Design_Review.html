SENIOR DESIGN 1 · LECTURE

End-of-Semester Design Review

What to present, and how, at the end of Senior Design 1

Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of Tulsa

THE REVIEW

What it is, and why it matters

WHAT

A formal 30-minute presentation

Faculty, your customer and guests may ask questions at any time during it.

WHY

Earn the spring

Demonstrate that the project is on track and ready to finish the detail design, build and test.

HOW

The whole team

Every member presents their own work. One member takes notes for the action items.

The burden is on your team to demonstrate that the project will succeed: it will meet the requirements, on schedule and within budget.

Senior Design 1 · End-of-Semester Design Review

02

BEFORE, DURING, AFTER

Run it like a professional review

Before

Invite your customer and at least three practicing engineers; send their names to the faculty

Practice, and list the questions you expect

During

Every member presents

Justify decisions with analysis or test results

One member writes down the action items

Expect tough questions; answer, don’t argue

After

Turn the action items into tasks, and add them to the WBS

Maintain the action item list: an owner, a due date and a deliverable for each
