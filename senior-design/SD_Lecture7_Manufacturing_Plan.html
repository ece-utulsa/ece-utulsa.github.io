WHY PLAN THE BUILD

The plan answers three questions

QUALITY

Build it right

Inspection at every step, so a bad part is caught on the bench, not in final test

SCHEDULE

Build it on time

Lead times, shop queues and build hours are tasks in the WBS

OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY

Build it safely

Trained people, the right tools, and the shop’s rules

A prototype that cannot be built from your drawings is a design problem, and the plan is where you find it.
