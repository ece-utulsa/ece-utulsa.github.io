THE FIRST RULE

State what it must do, not how

REQUIREMENT · WHAT

DESIGN · HOW

The system shall measure distance from 0.2 to 4.0 m to within ±1 cm.

We will use an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor.

The system shall report board temperature to the surface once per second.

We will send temperature over RS-485 from an STM32.

The system shall run for 8 hours between charges.

We will use a 5,000 mAh lithium-ion battery.

A requirement states the need. The design, chosen later, is one way to meet it.

WRITING A REQUIREMENT

Shall, should, will, and no vague words

shall

A binding requirement

should

A goal: desired, not binding

will

A statement of fact or intent

The display should be fast.

The display shall update within 100 ms of a sensor change.

The board shall be small and light.

The board shall fit in 50 × 300 mm and weigh under 250 g.

The system shall be user friendly.

A new operator shall start a run in under 2 minutes, unaided.
