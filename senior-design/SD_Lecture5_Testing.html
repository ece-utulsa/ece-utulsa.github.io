WHY TEST

Testing answers three questions

Performance

Will the design meet the customer’s requirements?

Will the parts we chose perform as the design needs?

Safety

Will the design be safe, in normal use and when things go wrong?

Will it stay safe across its whole environment?

Robustness

Will it keep performing over its life cycle?

Will it stand up to abuse?

If you are not measuring something, it is not a real test. And yes, software is tested too.
