IN INDUSTRY

These are real engineering careers

Design engineer

Creates new products from concept to released drawings, often as a new product development engineer.

Test engineer

Designs the tests that prove a product works and lasts: burn-in, highly accelerated life testing (HALT) and more.

Manufacturing engineer

Designs and runs the system that builds the product: fastest and cheapest, while keeping staff safe and quality high.

Each is a career in its own right. Your Senior Design role is a first job in it.
